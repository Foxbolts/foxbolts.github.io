- Rapid prototyping means building a quick, limited version of an idea so we can test it, get feedback, and decide whether to improve it or move on. - As you can see, we have plenty of ideas. The question is which ideas hold value? - Rapid prototyping itself isn’t new. So what has changed?
- We now have capable AI models that can help us build a first pass in the actual project at a speed and cost that make more experiments practical. - Before we look at a real example, let’s agree on what to expect from the code they produce.
